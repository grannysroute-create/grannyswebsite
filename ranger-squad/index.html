The Heart Behind the Books

The Story Behind the Squad

Granny's stories grew from miles traveled with Pappy Terry, memories of camping with the grandkids, and the five-jar habit they loved: Save some, Fix some, Fun some, Give some, and Invest some.

Pappy's favorite was the Giving jar. That spirit of generosity, family, adventure, and paying it forward is woven into The $5 Ranger Squad.
